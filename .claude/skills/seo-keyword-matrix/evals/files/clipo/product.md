# Product

Eval fixture: a made-up product for the seo-keyword-matrix evals. Not a real product.

## Positioning

Clipo turns product photos into short product videos with AI. Sellers upload 1–5 photos of one product and get a 10–15 second video in 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts) or 1:1 (Etsy, Shopify product pages).

## Target Users

Small e-commerce sellers on Etsy and Shopify who have product photos but no time or skill to film or edit video.

## Current Scope

- Photos → video: 1–5 photos, AI camera motion, 6 style templates, background music from a licensed library.
- Output: MP4, 9:16 or 1:1, 1080p, max 15 seconds.
- Credits: one video costs 1 credit. Credit Packs, no subscription.

## What We Are NOT Building

- A timeline video editor (no trimming, layers or multi-clip editing).
- AI avatars, talking heads or voice-over.
- Product photography or photo editing tools.
